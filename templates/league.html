Why Equitable Normalization is Vital

Raw aggregate kilowatt-hours inherently bias against larger residences: Block-C and D accommodate 240 students across 3,200 m², whereas Block-A and B house 180 students across 2,400 m². The Fair League evaluates Specific Energy Consumption (kWh per occupant-day) and Energy Use Intensity (EUI in kWh/m²/month) so student blocks compete on true behavioral and operational efficiency.
