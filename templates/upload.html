Expected CSV Schema

Your CSV file must include these column headers:

timestamp, block_id, power_kw, occupancy, temperature
• timestamp: YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS format
• block_id: Block-A, Block-B, Block-C, or Block-D
• power_kw: Numeric active power (≥ 0.0 kW)
• occupancy: Integer occupant count (≥ 0)
• temperature: Ambient temperature in °C (-20 to 65)
• Optional: floor, humidity, day_type
